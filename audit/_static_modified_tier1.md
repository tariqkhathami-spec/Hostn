## Patterns

- **Arabic-first hardcoding combined with selective `t()`** — dashboard mixes both, while reservations/[id], pricing/*, financial/*, property/unit/[id] and calendar are essentially Arabic-only
- **Status enum drift** — at least four places enumerate different overlapping subsets of `confirmed/pending/in_payment/waiting/held/cancelled/no_show/completed/rejected/paid`; combined with StatusBadge `||` fallback to typoed `منتظي`, hosts will see misleading labels
- **Single-property/single-unit assumptions** — `(tabs)/properties` (only `properties[0]`), `(tabs)/calendar` (`propertyName` is `calendarGroups[0].propertyName`), and `property/unit/[id]` (no-op filter returns all bookings)
- **Dead pressables**: pricing's "Add Offer", reservations tab's search/info/filter-arrow, calendar tab's month nav
- **Routes routinely cast `as any`**, suggesting the routes either don't exist or aren't typed
- **Null-deref risks** on common path: `formatArabicDate(undefined)`, `formatCurrency(booking.hostAmount)` when undefined
